The Raven That Cast No Shadow

The Chronicles of Renn — A Tale from the Forest of Fyor

By Lucas Montenegro

Synopsis

Deep within the ancient Forest of Fyor lies a hidden settlement where half-elves have found refuge from a world that once rejected them.

Baranion Finrod, a young half-elf dreaming of becoming a guardian, eagerly awaits the day he can form a bond with a magical companion. But his life takes an unexpected turn when he discovers an injured black raven with silver eyes and an impossible secret: it casts no shadow.

Determined to save the mysterious creature, Baranion soon finds himself caught in a series of unsettling events. Shadows begin moving on their own, ancient protective barriers weaken, and a forgotten path leads toward ruins that conceal a tragedy buried for generations.

With the help of his friends, Baranion must uncover the truth behind the raven's existence and confront a mystery born from love, desperation, and a bond that was never meant to endure.

But some bonds cannot be broken without sacrifice, and not every creature wishes to be saved.

A tale of friendship, mystery, and the courage to let go, set in the vast fantasy world of Renn.
